# Privacy Policy — Kharcha

**Last updated:** April 6, 2026

Kharcha is a personal expense tracking app built by Chetan Jain.

## Data Collection

Kharcha stores all your financial data **locally on your device** using SQLite. No data is sent to any server by default.

### What is stored locally

- Transaction records (amount, merchant, date, category)
- Budget and subscription settings
- App preferences and configuration

## Third-Party Services

Kharcha does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Data Sharing

Kharcha does **not** sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

## Data Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
